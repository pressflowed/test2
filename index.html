Game mode

Controls
  • Move: push the ground down and back with your hands. Fast strokes, then let go, to leap. Push down on walls to climb.
  • Smash: punch something hard and straight on to break a dent in it. Bigger players and harder punches break more. The floor and the outer wall never break.
  • Hide and seek: one player is a giant, everyone else is tiny and fast. Hiders get 30 seconds to hide while the giant waits in the dark. Then the giant hunts: only the giant can smash, and touching a hider catches them. The giant wins by catching everyone; the hiders win if anyone lasts 3 minutes.
  • Arm menu: it floats above your left hand. Poke its tabs and buttons with your right fingertip. To open or close it, poke the small Menu tab under it, or press X. The Game tab switches mode.
  • Sandbox, spawning: on the Items tab, reach into a preview with your right hand, squeeze grip to pull it out, and throw it. Right trigger quick-spawns, A picks the next item, B undoes, left trigger deletes, grip grabs props.
  • Also: right stick snap-turns, Y takes you back to the start (sandbox).
  • Desktop: hold left / right click and drag down to push; Space for both hands. J punches what you look at. O switches mode. F throws the selected item, wheel or 1–8 pick one, V quick-spawns, G (hold) grabs, X deletes, Z undoes. M shows the menu, T switches tab, [ ] size, - = power, L gravity, N sky, H smashing on/off, Backspace repairs the map, Q / E turn, R resets, Esc pauses.
