Canopy Tag

Movement test room. No legs: push off the world with your hands.

In VR

  • Push the ground down and back to move. Fast strokes, then let go, to leap.
  • Push down on walls and posts to climb.
  • A / X: calibrate. Stand up straight with your arms relaxed, then press.
  • B / Y: back to start. Right stick: snap turn.

On desktop

  • Hold left / right click and drag the mouse down to push with that hand. Space uses both.
  • Mouse looks around. Q / E turn. R resets. Esc pauses.
