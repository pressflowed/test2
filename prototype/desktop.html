Canopy Tag

No legs. Just arms. Push off the world to move, and don't let the infected touch you.

  • MouseLook around
  • Hold L / R clickGrab with your left or right hand, then move the mouse to swing that hand
  • Hold SpaceGrab with both hands at once
  • Q / E, arrowsTurn while your hands are busy
  • RStart a new round
  • EscPause

How to run: look at the ground a little ahead, hold left click and drag the mouse down fast, then let go. Switch to right click and do it again. Fast strokes launch you. Push down on a wall or tree to climb it.

Needs a mouse. Best in fullscreen.
